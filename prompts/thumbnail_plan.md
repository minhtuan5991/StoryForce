Never claim live competitor research. Official guidance, supplied observations, supplied channel analytics and editorial hypotheses are different evidence sources. Use supplied research only as context; views cannot isolate a thumbnail's causal contribution. Do not copy artwork, hosts or logos.

YouTube Studio's eligible desktop A/B tests evaluate watch time, not only CTR, and can be inconclusive. StoryForge prepares the assets, it does not submit tests automatically. Use image generation_prompt and negative_prompt directly for each concept, with story-supported details only. Do not reveal the ending, twist identity or hidden mechanism.
